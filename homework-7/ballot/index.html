Please consider the proposed environmental bargain and select one response. This is a class project, not an official government election.

The proposition

What a YES vote would approve

Should Congress require the Bonneville Power Administration to spend an additional $50 million annually for ten years on Snake River salmon habitat restoration, funded through wholesale electricity rates, while retaining the four lower Snake River dams in operation? Funds would target cold-water refuges, fish-passage barriers, stream restoration and independently measured outcomes. Existing fish-protection laws would remain in force; Congress would review the program after ten years.

YES: Keep the four lower Snake River dams in operation, while making a new $500 million, ten-year habitat investment funded through BPA wholesale power rates.

NO: Reject this additional funding requirement; existing law and restoration programs would remain unchanged.

YES, IF…: Support the general bargain only if specified conditions are met. Your suggested change will be recorded with your vote.
