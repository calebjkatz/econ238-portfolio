# Mine ≠ Magnet

ECON 238 student-created SHOW ME investigation by Caleb Katz.

Public exhibit: https://calebjkatz.github.io/econ238-portfolio/homework-5/rare-earths/

The exhibit asks why the United States can be a meaningful rare-earth miner while still depending on a supply chain heavily concentrated in China. It separates mining from oxide separation, metal/alloy refining, and magnet manufacturing; reconstructs the U.S. industry's decline; explains magnet-relevant lanthanides; and distinguishes current operating capacity from projects that are only under construction, at pilot scale, or in award negotiations.

Primary evidence is from USGS, GAO, and DOE. Current MP Materials operational figures are clearly labeled as company-reported. Different-year supply-chain shares are labeled as stage-specific benchmarks rather than a synchronized snapshot.
