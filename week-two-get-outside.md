## Sources

- [Great New York State Fair — Daily Attendance](https://nysfair.ny.gov/about/press/attendance/): 2026 attendance of 962,795 and historic attendance comparisons. NYS Fair Daily Attendance

- [NYS Department of Agriculture and Markets — 2019 Fair Survey](https://agriculture.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2020/06/exhibit2summaryofsurveyresults_rfp0231_marketingadvertisingpublicrelationsservicesnysfair.pdf): visitor origin, demographics, travel modes, motivations, spending and satisfaction. This is older data and should be labeled as 2019. Agriculture and Markets

- [Research & Marketing Strategies — 2026 Viewpoint Survey](https://rmsresults.com/2026/08/24/new-york-state-fair-2026-survey-food-concerts-attractions/): pre-Fair expected spending, travel, motivations and favorite attractions. Survey of 820 panel respondents conducted Aug. 7–17, 2026; it measures survey respondents' intentions, not final Fair attendee behavior. 2026 NYS Fair Survey

- [Great New York State Fair — Parking and Transportation](https://nysfair.ny.gov/your-visit/plan-your-trip/parking/): parking capacity, parking cost and Centro options. Parking and Transportation

- [U.S. EPA — Typical Passenger Vehicle Emissions](https://www.epa.gov/greenvehicles/greenhouse-gas-emissions-typical-passenger-vehicle): approximately 400 grams of tailpipe CO₂ per mile for the average passenger vehicle. EPA Vehicle Emissions

- [Great New York State Fair — Vendor Information and 2026 License Rates](https://nysfair.ny.gov/becoming-a-vendor/): number of competing vendors and Fair space costs. Becoming a Vendor

- [Office of the New York State Comptroller — Open Book New York](https://wwe2.osc.state.ny.us/transparency/contracts/contractresults.cfm?PageNum_rsContract=1&a=AGM01&ac=&b=Search&cn=&oc=3000216&order=CONTRACT_START_DATE&selCTDateChoice=0&selCTDateChoiceOperator=0&selContractAmountChoice=0&selOrigDateChoiceOperator=0&sort=DESC&txtCTFromDate=&txtCTToDate=&txtContractAmount1=&txtContractAmount2=&txtOrigFromDate=&txtOrigToDate=&v=&vo=B&): individual 2026 State Fair vendor/concession contracts. WWE Vendor Contract Data

- [NYS Agriculture and Markets — 2025 Fair by the Numbers](https://nysfair.ny.gov/the-fair-by-the-numbers-celebrating-the-2025-great-new-york-state-fair-with-fun-feats-and-fantastic-memories/): milk, doughnuts, agricultural programs and transportation statistics. Fair by the Numbers

- [NYS Fair — 2026 Taste NY](https://nysfair.ny.gov/taste-ny-program-at-the-great-new-york-state-fair-showcases-agriculture-local-food-and-beverages/): more than 250 products from over 80 participating vendors in the Pop-Up Market and more than 30 New York producers in the Marketplace. Taste NY Program

- [NYS Department of Health — Fair food safety](https://www.health.ny.gov/press/releases/2025/2025-08-26_ny_state_fair.htm): vendor permitting and inspections during the Fair. NY State Health Commission

- [NYS Agriculture and Markets — Fairgrounds Trash Contract](https://agriculture.ny.gov/ifb-0370-trash-removal-and-disposal-new-york-state-fairgrounds): waste streams explicitly include recyclables, food waste and compostable materials. Trash Removal and Disposal

- [U.S. EPA — Environmental Impacts of Food Waste / Landfill Methane](https://www.epa.gov/land-research/farm-kitchen-environmental-impacts-us-food-waste): national food-waste and landfill context. These figures describe the United States overall, not the State Fair specifically. EPA Food Waste

- [NYS Agriculture and Markets / NYPA — Fairgrounds energy improvements and GreenFair](https://www.dot.ny.gov/news/press-releases/2019/2019-08-21): LED upgrades, waste initiatives and renewable-energy plans. NY State DOT Press Release

- [New York FY 2026–27 Enacted Budget](https://assembly.ny.gov/2026budget/2026_bills/enacted/A10000d.pdf?v=1779876613): approximately $30.923 million State Fair Program appropriation; this covers the State Fair program/year-round operation and should not be described as the cost of the thirteen-day Fair alone. NY State Budget

- [NYS Agriculture and Markets 2019 Annual Report](https://agriculture.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2020/06/2019-annual-report.pdf): historical estimate of more than $100 million in Syracuse-area economic activity and details on year-round Fairgrounds use. Agriculture and Markets Report 2019

- [Iowa State Fair — 2026 Attendance](https://www.iowastatefair.org/about/fair-dates-attendance): 1,218,834 over eleven days. Iowa State Fair Attendance

- [Minnesota State Fair — 2026 Attendance](https://www.mnstatefair.org/updates/2026-fair): 1,923,325 over twelve days; Minnesota also states that its Fair is financially self-supporting. Minnesota State Fair Attendance

- [State Fair of Texas — 2025 Attendance](https://bigtex.com/about-us/daily-attendance/): 2,020,064 over twenty-four days. Texas State Fair Attendance
